Crop-specific guidance

Critical stages differ by crop. Cotton needs special care at flowering and boll formation; wheat at crown-root initiation, flowering and grain filling; maize at tasseling and silking; onion and tomato prefer frequent, controlled irrigation. Avoid waterlogging in soybean, tur, groundnut and chickpea. These planning estimates should be adjusted using local soil observations and agricultural-extension advice.

Method note: ETc = Open-Meteo FAO reference ET₀ × stage crop coefficient; 80% of forecast rain is treated as effective rainfall; gross depth accounts for method efficiency. Sensor-free results explicitly carry a limitation.
Smart estimates support—not replace—field inspection and local agronomic advice.
